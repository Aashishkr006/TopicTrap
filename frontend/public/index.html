Speaking Frameworks
STAR
Situation → Task → Action → Result
S = Situation: Set the scene
T = Task: Your responsibility
A = Action: What you did
R = Result: The outcome
PREP
Point → Reason → Example → Point
P = Point: Your main claim
R = Reason: Why it's true
E = Example: Specific evidence
P = Point: Restate it strongly
PPF
Past → Present → Future
P = Past: Where you've been
P = Present: Where you are
F = Future: Where you're headed
MECE
Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive
1. Break into distinct categories
2. No overlaps between buckets
3. Nothing left out
Best for case study questions
